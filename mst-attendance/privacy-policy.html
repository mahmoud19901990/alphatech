Privacy Policy – MST Attendance

Effective Date: August 10, 2026
Last Updated: August 10, 2026

MST Attendance (“the App”) is an employee attendance application designed to allow employees to securely record their attendance and allow authorized managers to manage attendance-related information.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information processed through MST Attendance. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why it is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

MST Attendance does not provide public user registration.

Employee accounts are created or provided by the employee's organization or authorized manager. When you sign in, the App may process information associated with your employee account, including:

  • Name and employee/account identifier
  • Username
  • Assigned roles and permissions
  • Assigned workplace or attendance areas
  • Authentication information necessary to sign in securely

Authentication credentials are used only for accessing the authorized MST Attendance account and related services.

Attendance Information

When you use MST Attendance, we process information necessary to maintain attendance records, which may include:

  • Clock-in date and time
  • Clock-out date and time
  • Attendance status
  • Workplace or assigned attendance area
  • Attendance history
  • Location associated with a Clock In or Clock Out action
  • Information required to validate the attendance request

This information is used to provide attendance functionality to you and your employer or organization.

2. Precise Location Information

MST Attendance uses your device's precise location only when it is required to perform a Clock In or Clock Out operation.

When you press Clock In or Clock Out, the App may request your current GPS location and send the necessary location information to the MST Attendance server.

The location is used to:

  • Confirm that you are within a workplace or attendance area assigned to you.
  • Prevent unauthorized Clock In or Clock Out operations from locations outside the permitted area.
  • Associate the attendance action with the location where it was performed.
  • Help maintain accurate and secure attendance records.

MST Attendance does not continuously track your location.

The App does not use continuous background location tracking to monitor your movements. Your location is requested in connection with an attendance action rather than continuously throughout your workday.

Location permission is therefore required when location verification is necessary to complete a Clock In or Clock Out operation.

Authorized managers may also use location functionality when defining or managing attendance areas.

3. Fingerprint and Biometric Authentication

MST Attendance may use biometric authentication, such as your fingerprint, to confirm a Clock In or Clock Out operation.

Biometric authentication provides an additional level of security and helps confirm that the attendance action is being performed by the authorized device user.

MST Attendance uses the biometric authentication capabilities provided by your device's operating system.

MST Attendance does not receive or store your actual fingerprint or biometric template.

Your fingerprint or other biometric information remains managed by the security system of your device. The App receives only the authentication result needed to determine whether the biometric verification was successful.

Depending on the device and its configuration, device authentication such as the device PIN, password, pattern, or passcode may also be used where supported.

4. Device Information

To help protect attendance records and prevent unauthorized attendance actions, MST Attendance may process limited information about the device being used.

This may include:

  • Device identifier used by MST Attendance
  • Device platform or operating system
  • Application version
  • Registered-device status
  • Technical information required for security and troubleshooting

Device information may be used to determine whether an employee is using an authorized or registered device.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through MST Attendance is used only as necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the attendance service. This includes:

  • Authenticating users
  • Recording Clock In and Clock Out operations
  • Verifying attendance locations
  • Verifying biometric or device authentication
  • Maintaining employee attendance history
  • Validating assigned attendance areas
  • Enforcing employee and manager permissions
  • Detecting or preventing unauthorized attendance activity
  • Providing attendance information to authorized managers
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the service
  • Troubleshooting technical problems

We do not use precise location information to continuously monitor employees.

6. Manager and Employer Access

MST Attendance is intended for use by organizations and their employees.

Authorized managers or administrators may have access to information necessary to manage employee attendance, including:

  • Employee attendance records
  • Clock-in and clock-out times
  • Attendance status
  • Attendance locations associated with attendance actions
  • Assigned attendance areas
  • Employee-to-area assignments
  • Registered-device information
  • Attendance history

Access depends on the permissions assigned to the manager or administrator.

Your employer or organization may also have its own policies regarding employee attendance records and their retention.

7. Data Storage and Security

Attendance and account information may be transmitted securely to the MST Attendance backend server and stored in the organization's attendance system.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Authentication and authorization mechanisms are used to restrict access to attendance information according to the user's account and permissions.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable safeguards are used to protect information processed by the service.

8. Location Permissions

You may control location permissions through your device settings.

Because precise location verification is an important security feature of MST Attendance, disabling location permission may prevent the App from completing Clock In or Clock Out operations that require workplace location verification.

MST Attendance does not require continuous location monitoring for normal attendance operation.

9. Biometric Permissions

Biometric authentication is handled through the authentication features provided by your device.

MST Attendance does not independently collect, store, or process your fingerprint image or biometric template.

You can manage fingerprints, biometric authentication, and device security settings through your device's operating system settings.

10. Data Sharing

MST Attendance does not sell users' personal information.

Attendance information is processed for the purpose of providing the attendance service and may be accessible to the employer, organization, managers, administrators, or authorized service operators as required to provide and maintain the service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.

11. Data Retention

Attendance information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the attendance service, maintain business and employment records, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain system security.

The specific retention period may also depend on the policies and legal obligations of the organization using MST Attendance.

12. Employee Privacy

MST Attendance is designed to minimize location collection.

  • The App does not continuously monitor an employee's location.
  • The App does not use location information to create a continuous history of an employee's movements.
  • Precise location is requested when required for Clock In or Clock Out verification.
  • Biometric verification is performed by the device's operating system.
  • Actual fingerprint or biometric templates are not stored by MST Attendance.

13. Children's Privacy

MST Attendance is a business and employee attendance application and is not designed or intended for use by children.

The service is intended for authorized employees and managers of organizations using MST Attendance.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to MST Attendance, security practices, legal requirements, or the way information is processed.

When the Privacy Policy is updated, the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document will be changed accordingly.
